Jeeva Herbs - Delivery Information

Customers may contact support for questions about their order, shipping, tracking, or delivery delays.

Before answering a delivery question:
1. Verify the customer's order using the authenticated order details.
2. Check the actual order status before providing an update.
3. Do not invent tracking details or delivery dates.
4. If the order is delayed or the status cannot be verified, create or escalate a support ticket for the team to review.

Delivery time, shipping charges, cancellation rules and return eligibility must be confirmed using the current business policy or the customer's order information. Do not promise a refund, replacement, or delivery date without confirmation.
