Jeeva Herbs - Payment Support Information

Customers may contact support about payment failures, duplicate payments, payment confirmation, or refunds.

Payment Support Guidelines:
1. Check the actual order and payment records before confirming payment status.
2. Never ask customers to share their password, OTP, CVV, or complete card details.
3. Do not claim that a refund has been processed unless the actual payment records confirm it.
4. If a payment was deducted but the order was not confirmed, create a support ticket for the team to investigate.
5. Refund timelines and payment-provider rules must be confirmed from the current business policy before communicating them to a customer.
